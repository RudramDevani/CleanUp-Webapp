About CleanUp Surat

Together for a Cleaner, Greener Surat

Who We Are

A city-wide movement, led by citizens

CleanUp Surat brings together volunteers, schools, housing societies and companies who believe a clean city is built by the people who live in it.

From weekend cleanup drives to adopting public spaces and reporting waste hotspots, we turn small actions into lasting change across Surat.

Join the Movement
🎯

Our Mission

Mobilise citizens to keep Surat clean through action, awareness and accountability.

🌅

Our Vision

A Surat where every street, lake and garden is cared for by the people who use it.
